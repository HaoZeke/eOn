`job = instanton` with `mode = rate` computes the ring-polymer instanton rate through a first-order saddle below the crossover temperature: Newton eigenvector following on the block-cyclic ring Hessian with Bofill-updated bead blocks (one batch of N/2 + 1 force calls per step), a ring seeded from a band by the period condition or from the previous temperature (`temperatures`), the prefactor from recomputed or updated bead Hessians through a block LU without the dense matrix, and per-temperature rows in `rate_instanton.dat` beside harmonic TST and the one-dimensional WKB rate along the path. Validated against the exact Eckart flux and the Caldeira and Leggett decay rate.
